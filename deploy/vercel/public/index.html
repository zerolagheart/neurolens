Explainable MRI classification · research demo

NeuroLens

Upload a single brain MRI slice to see how a model trained on the public Nickparvar Brain Tumor MRI dataset scores it across four dataset labels, how confident it is, and which regions drove its prediction (Grad-CAM).

Research and educational use only. NeuroLens is not a medical device and its output is not a medical diagnosis. Image-level classification does not localise or segment tumours, and model scores are not clinically validated. Do not upload identifiable patient data.
